# Slackademics — Rulebook

## Overview

Slackademics is a 1–4 player card game (1 human vs 3 AI) set in a fictional university. You and your fellow students must scrape through 8 semesters, doing just enough work to pass your exams while still finding time to party. Too many fails and you get expelled. Most party points at the end wins.

---

## Players & Roles

- **4 players** — 1 human, 3 AI opponents (Alex, Billie, Casey).
- Each semester, one player is the **Project Leader (PL)**. The PL rotates each semester.
- The PL controls the effort pile and decides whether to Let It Ride or use a skill.

---

## The Decks

### Effort Deck
Cards numbered 1–8. Shuffled at the start of each semester. Every player draws a hand of cards from the effort deck.

### Fail / Extra Credit Deck
Contains **Fail** cards and **Extra Credit** cards. Drawn when players receive fails or extra credit tokens.

---

## Game Structure

The game lasts **8 semesters**. Each semester:

1. A **Project Card** is drawn — it shows the effort target needed to pass.
2. Players play effort cards and party cards.
3. The effort pile is revealed on **Day of Deadline**.
4. The group either **passes** or **fails** the exam.
5. A **Blame Vote** always follows — win or lose.
6. End-of-semester cleanup happens, then the next semester begins.

---

## Semester Flow

### 1. Card Selection Phase
Each player simultaneously chooses:
- **One effort card** to contribute to the shared effort pile (played face-down).
- **One party card** to add to their own party pile (played face-down).

The AI selects cards automatically based on their assigned personality.

### 2. Day of Deadline
The PL reveals cards from the effort pile one at a time.

After each reveal, the PL chooses:
- **Let It Ride** — flip the next card and keep going.
- **Use a Leadership Skill** (if available) — apply a special effect before the next flip.

Special cards in the effort pile:
- **X2 Copy** — doubles the total effort so far when revealed.

Once all cards are revealed, the total is compared to the project target.

### 3. Outcome
- **Pass** — the group passes! The exam card is placed face-up at the top of the effort track (used for expansion scoring).
- **Fail** — the group fails. The exam card is placed face-down at the top of the effort track. **All players receive 1 Group Fail.**

A Blame Vote follows regardless of outcome.

### 4. Blame Vote
All players (including the PL) vote on who to blame for the semester's result.

- Each player casts 1 vote for another player.
- The player with the most votes receives a **Personal Fail**.
- Ties are broken by a spin — the arrow lands on one of the tied players.

**Personal Fail:** The blamed player takes 1 Personal Fail token and places it next to their party pile face-up.

### 5. End of Semester
- Players who received a Personal Fail this semester must **discard one party card** from their party pile.
- Hands are replenished for the next semester.
- The Project Leader role passes to the next player.

---

## Fails & Expulsion

There are two types of fails:

| Type | Who gets it | When |
|------|-------------|------|
| **Group Fail** | All players | When the group fails an exam |
| **Personal Fail** | One player | When blamed after the vote |

**Total Fails** = Group Fails + Personal Fails.

A player is **expelled** when their total fails reach **5**. Expelled players:
- Are out of the game.
- Keep their current party pile (their score is locked in).
- No longer participate in card selection, blame votes, or snitching.

---

## Snitching

During the Blame Vote phase, any non-PL player may choose to **snitch** on another player — accusing them of deliberately playing a low effort card.

**Snitch succeeds** if the accused player's effort card was the lowest in the pile:
- The accused player receives a **Personal Fail**.
- The snitcher gains **+1 party card** from the deck.

**Snitch fails** if the accused player's effort card was not the lowest:
- The snitcher must **discard 1 party card** from their party pile as a penalty.
- If the snitcher has no party card to discard, no penalty is applied.

---

## Leadership Skills

The Project Leader may have skill cards available during Day of Deadline. Skills are drawn at the start of the semester and are single-use.

Skills can:
- Boost the current effort total.
- Remove a card from the pile.
- Extend the deadline (add extra effort cards).
- Other special effects.

The AI uses skills strategically — only when the effort pile is likely to fall short.

---

## Semester 6 — Free Break

In Semester 6, instead of the normal card selection phase, each player gets a **free pick** of any two cards from the full 1–8 effort deck to add to their party pile. No exam occurs this semester.

---

## Extra Credit

Some Leadership Skills or events award **Extra Credit** cards.

- Extra Credit cards are worth **+3 party points** at the end of the game.
- Extra Credit can sometimes be gifted to another player.
- Players who finish the game with **0 total fails** (group + personal) receive a **Responsible Bonus** of +3 Extra Credit.

---

## Scoring & End Game

After 8 semesters, each player counts their **party pile**:

- Each numbered party card = face value in points.
- **X2 Copy** cards = double the running total when scored.
- Each **Extra Credit** card = +3 points.
- Expelled players score only what they accumulated before expulsion.

The player with the **highest party pile score** wins.

---

## AI Personalities

When starting a new game, you can assign an AI personality to each opponent:

| Personality | Behaviour |
|-------------|-----------|
| **Balanced** | Mixes strategies; contributes reasonable effort, parties moderately. May surprise you. |
| **Random** | Picks every card completely at random. Pure chaos — can accidentally save or sink the group. |
| **Greedy** | Strongly prefers playing low cards to the effort pile and high cards to their party pile. Will let the group fail to hoard points. |

---

## Quick Reference

| Situation | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| Exam fails | All players +1 Group Fail |
| Blame vote winner | +1 Personal Fail; discard 1 party card at end of semester |
| 5 total fails (group + personal) | Expelled |
| Snitch correct | Accused +1 Personal Fail; snitcher +1 party card |
| Snitch wrong | Snitcher discards 1 party card (no fail) |
| Semester 6 | Free pick: any 2 cards to party pile; no exam |
| 0 total fails at game end | +3 Extra Credit (Responsible Bonus) |
| Extra Credit card | +3 party points |
